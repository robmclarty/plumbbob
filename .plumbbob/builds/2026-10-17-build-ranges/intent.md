## Frame

- **Problem:** Rob drives long builds by typing step ranges (`/plumbbob:build 31-39`), and plumbbob has no idea which ranges make sense. On 2026-10-16 a model wrote the answer into three builds by hand, at his request: which ranges to type, and where each one stops because a human call is due, either a decision the plan could not make on paper or a review before later steps build on what landed. Nothing reads those sections. The plan skill does not write one, the template has no place for one, and the card still points one step at a time (`**Next Up**: Step 31 of 78`), so the run plan lives in the human's head, and a range that ends gives no word on why it ended there. The same build logs show the grant failing in practice. Twice a background job's completion notice reached the prompt hook as a human turn and cleared the grant partway through a range (checkride's 48-57 at step 49, fascicle's 10-12 at step 11). Twice the build order broke a range, because a range names plan numbers and an appended step sat inside it: checkride's 48-57 could reach only 51 with step 76 next, and fascicle's run of "steps 20 and 5 to 9" had to be typed as two.
- **Smallest thing that solves it:** a `## Build ranges` section the plan writes beside `## Build order`, one numbered item per range (the command to type, the stop that ends it, an optional model); a pure parser that works out where the build stands in it; a segment beneath Next Up naming the range to type next, and a Stop line on the boundary where a range's last step lands, saying why the human is stopped there; the ranges on the dashboard, with any the plan has outgrown flagged; and the plan, step, refine, order, harvest, and build skills keeping the section true. The build also stops a background job's notice from counting as a turn ([D11 (notice-not-turn)](#d11)), lets a range list its steps ([D12 (step-list-grants)](#d12)), and halts `--auto` at a call the plan reserves for the human ([D14 (auto-and-stops)](#d14)).
- **Done looks like:** a fresh `/plumbbob:plan` writes a section that parses and names every step once; at the plan pause, the plan commit, a step pause, and every boundary, the card names the range to type next (`/plumbbob:build 6-9`) or what is left of the one in progress; when a range's last step lands, the boundary says why the human is stopped there; `plumbbob status` lists the ranges with the one in progress marked, and names a range the plan or the build order has outgrown; a plan without the section renders exactly as it does today; from step 7 on, this build's own card names this build's own ranges; and `pnpm check` is green.
- **Explicitly NOT doing:** a range that starts without the human typing it (no chaining from one range to the next, and no `looks good` that starts one); the CLI deciding where a range stops; ranking models to pick a range's; a verb that writes the section; recording range runs in `stats.json` or the finish report; a new eval contract; editing another build's record, here or in another repo, so checkride's live `## Ranges` and fascicle's `## Build runs` keep parsing as no ranges; a version bump or CHANGELOG entry.

## Decisions

- <a id="d1"></a>**D1 (plan-holds-ranges)**: the ranges live in `intent.md` under `## Build ranges`, right after `## Build order`, *because* the plan is already the tracked document the skills edit and the parser reads, which is why plumbbob [D86 (build-order)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d86) put the sequence there, and all three hand-written sections already sit in a plan.
- <a id="d2"></a>**D2 (range-grammar)**: a range is one numbered item, `` `/plumbbob:build 6-9`, **stop:** <what the human decides or reviews there> ``, with an optional `- model:` sub-line and any other sub-line (a `- note:` on what to watch for) left to the reader, *because* the Steps grammar already reads this way, an item wraps where a table row of prose runs off the screen, and the item's number is the handle the card counts by.
- <a id="d3"></a>**D3 (form-says-where)**: the command's form says where a range stops: a bare `N` builds step N to its pause, so its stop is a call made before the step lands, and a range lands every step it names and stops at the boundary after the last, so its stop is a review of what landed, *because* that is what the two forms already do (plumbbob [D85 (range-top-lands)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d85)), so a stop needs no kind of its own.
- <a id="d4"></a>**D4 (advice-not-grant)**: a range in the section grants nothing, and the human still types the command, *because* a grant comes only from the human's literal prompt (plumbbob [D65 (human-typed-grants)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d65)), and a line the model can edit would be a grant the model could forge (plumbbob [D67 (auto-not-a-grant)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d67)).
- <a id="d5"></a>**D5 (one-grammar)**: the section's command and the grant the hook mints from a typed range are read by one parser, *because* a card that reads a range one way while the hook reads it another would suggest a command that does something else.
- <a id="d6"></a>**D6 (state-is-derived)**: a range's progress is read from its steps' boxes and never stored: done when every step it names is `[x]`, in progress when it holds the next undone step in build order, *because* the boxes are already the truth, and a second copy of a fact is one more thing to drift.
- <a id="d7"></a>**D7 (plan-places-stops)**: the plan decides where ranges stop and the CLI never does: a range ends where a human call is due (a decision the plan leaves open, a review before later steps build on what landed, or something only the human can do, such as a push, a live key, or spending money) and nowhere else, *because* where a call falls is judgment, and all three hand-written sections cut at exactly those points.
- <a id="d8"></a>**D8 (model-declared)**: a range's model is what its own `- model:` line says, never computed from its steps, *because* a ranking of model names in code goes stale with the next release, the same reason tokens and cost stay out of the `spent` row.
- <a id="d9"></a>**D9 (informative-only)**: each new card and dashboard line renders only when the plan declares ranges and the line says something the card does not already hold, *because* a reminder that repeats what is on screen is noise (plumbbob [D86 (build-order)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d86) made the same call for its line), and a plan without the section has to render exactly as it does today.
- <a id="d10"></a>**D10 (drift-reported)**: a range the plan has outgrown is reported and never rewritten by the CLI: one naming a step the plan lacks, a step two ranges both name, an undone step no range names, or an undone step outside a range that the build order places between two of the range's own; the dashboard names it, `recover` names one that points at a step the plan lacks (as it does for the build order), and the card suggests no command for it, *because* only the human knows where the stop belongs now, and a suggested command that halts early is the failure this build exists to remove.
- <a id="d11"></a>**D11 (notice-not-turn)**: a background job's completion notice is not a human turn: for a prompt step 1's rule marks as a notice, the prompt hook leaves `TURN` and `GRANT` as they were and emits no nudge, *because* the latch reads `TURN` passing a step's entry stamp as its only sign that a human answered, and two ranges this week lost their grant to a Stryker run's notice (settles [Q1 (notice-not-turn)](#q1)).
- <a id="d12"></a>**D12 (step-list-grants)**: a range may list its steps, numbers and spans joined by commas (`20,5-9`); the hook mints exactly that set, the latch lands only its members, and a plain `N-M` grants exactly N through M, *because* the set is fixed by what the human typed, so no later edit to the build order can widen it, where reading `N-M` along the build order would let the model widen a grant by reordering the plan (settles [Q2 (step-list-grants)](#q2)).
- <a id="d13"></a>**D13 (card-shape)**: the note beneath Next Up opens with the range holding the step Next Up names and the command that runs what is left of it (`· range 3 of 5: /plumbbob:build 6-9`), or says the range needs a re-cut, and why, when [D10 (drift-reported)](#d10) flags it; a `**Stop**: Range 3 of 5 landed - <its stop text>` part sits between the advisories and Next Up on the boundary where a landing range's last step lands, and nowhere else; and the dashboard lists the ranges under the steps (`ranges  2 of 5 done`, with ✓ and ▸), a drift row per flagged range, and a `next →` that names the range's command, *because* those are the two questions the human brings to a boundary, what to type next and why the build stopped, and a bare range's stop is its own pause, where the injected dashboard already puts the stop text in front of the model writing the recommendation (settles [Q3 (card-shape)](#q3); the exact strings are step 5's).
- <a id="d14"></a>**D14 (auto-and-stops)**: under `--auto`, a step the ranges name as a bare range is built to its pause and halts there at the decision tier, with `plumbbob build` saying so as it enters the step; a review stop is run through, a typed range stays as typed, and the halt is the build notice's and the skill's, never the latch's, *because* a plan that reserves a call for the human should not have it made by a flag typed for speed, a review can wait for the end of the run, and the ranges are advice under [D4 (advice-not-grant)](#d4) (settles [Q4 (auto-and-stops)](#q4)).
- <a id="d15"></a>**D15 (notice-clears-relay)**: a notice also clears `RELAY`, through a `clearRelay` beside `clearPause` in `src/lib/sidecar.ts`, *because* the ending in it was judged when its own turn ended, a hold mark would carry forward within the `TURN` (`writeRelay`, `src/lib/sidecar.ts:728`) and wave through any ending printed after the notice, and a fresh record is checked as usual (settles [Q5 (notice-and-relay)](#q5)).
- <a id="d16"></a>**D16 (list-reading)**: once any token in the argument run carries a comma or a dash, every number and span in the run joins one set, and bare numbers alone mint nothing; a range starts at its first undone step in build order, however it is written; and the card writes a list in build order (`20,5-9`, `48-51,76,52-57`), *because* `20, 5-9` with a space reads today as `range 9`, which refuses the range's first step, and a set has no order of its own, so the build order has to supply it (settles [Q6 (list-reading)](#q6)).
- <a id="d17"></a>**D17 (range-model)**: while the note suggests a range, the bold model in Next Up's bracket is the range's and the note names no model; otherwise the bracket keeps the step's, *because* the range's model is the `/model` the human picks before typing it, and a range's first step is often not its hardest (settles [Q7 (range-model)](#q7)).
- <a id="d18"></a>**D18 (scaffold-range)**: step 5's spec fixes the template's placeholder range; an item whose command or stop still holds a `<...>` fill-in reads as scaffold, neither a range nor a report; and [D10 (drift-reported)](#d10)'s coverage check runs only once at least one real range parses, *because* a fresh build would otherwise warn on its dashboard and flag every step, and plumbbob [D77 (placeholder-uncounted)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d77) settled the same case for the scaffolded question (settles [Q8 (scaffold-range)](#q8)).

## Constraints

- <a id="c1"></a>**C1 (no-new-deps)**: no new dependency of any kind.
- <a id="c2"></a>**C2 (hooks-never-wedge)**: every hook path (`turn`, `relay`) still exits 0 and degrades to no output on any failure.
- <a id="c3"></a>**C3 (records-stay-put)**: no file in another build's folder is edited, here or in another repo; a plan written before this build keeps parsing as no ranges.
- <a id="c4"></a>**C4 (spec-first)**: a change to a turn's shape lands in `docs/presentation.md` before the code that renders it, per plumbbob [D80 (cli-renders-model-relays)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d80).
- <a id="c5"></a>**C5 (no-release)**: no version bump and no CHANGELOG entry; the release is cut by hand with `/version`.
- <a id="c6"></a>**C6 (temp-repo-dogfood)**: hand-run checks use `node src/cli.ts` in a `mktemp -d` repo, never a stale `dist/` and never this repo's live session.
- <a id="c7"></a>**C7 (house-voice)**: authored prose follows `docs/voice/` with no em-dash (plumbbob [D78 (em-dash-ban)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d78)), relayed CLI strings keep theirs, and every citation carries its slug (plumbbob [D74 (glossed-citations)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d74)).
- <a id="c8"></a>**C8 (old-grants-land)**: the latch keeps honoring a `range M` grant, ceiling and all, *because* this repo's own prompt hook is the installed plugin's until a release, and this build's ranges have to land while it runs.
- <a id="c9"></a>**C9 (numbers-for-life)**: nothing renumbers a step, and a range names plan numbers (plumbbob [D86 (build-order)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d86)).

## Build ranges

Five commands drive the build, in the grammar plumbbob [D92 (build-ranges)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d92) settled at step 5. Two stops are calls made at a step's own pause (the notice rule at step 1, the card's exact wording at step 5), and three are reviews after a range lands. Each range names the strongest model any step inside it recommends.

1. `/plumbbob:build 1`, **stop:** at step 1's pause, read the captured payloads and approve the rule that tells a background job's notice from a typed prompt, or settle what step 2 becomes if nothing does
   - model: opus
2. `/plumbbob:build 2-4`, **stop:** review the latch before anything builds on it, since the latch is the one guarantee plumbbob enforces: a notice no longer counts as a turn, and a range lands only the steps it names
   - model: opus
   - note: this repo's prompt hook is the installed plugin's until the fix ships in a release, so step 2 does not protect this build; run long jobs (a sweep, a mutation run) in the foreground while a range is live.
3. `/plumbbob:build 5`, **stop:** at step 5's pause, approve the spec's exact wording for the range segment, the bracket's model, the Stop part, the dashboard block, and the template's placeholder before steps 6 to 10 render them, and confirm this section's grammar holds
   - model: fable
4. `/plumbbob:build 6-10`, **stop:** review the card on this build's own boundary, where step 10's checkpoint should print this range's stop and name range 5 as the next command
   - model: opus
5. `/plumbbob:build 11-13`, **stop:** the final read of the skills and docs, then `/plumbbob:finish`
   - model: opus

## Open questions

- <a id="q1"></a>**Q1 (notice-not-turn)**: *resolved:* 2026-10-17, the lean, recorded as [D11 (notice-not-turn)](#d11); the question was whether this build stops a background job's completion notice from counting as a human turn
  - *plain:* The prompt hook ticks `TURN` and rewrites `GRANT` on every prompt it sees, and two build logs from this week say a background job's notice reaches it as one. Fascicle's range 10-12 lost its grant at step 11 when a Stryker run reported back, and checkride's 48-57 lost its grant at step 49 the same way; each time the model rightly refused to rewrite the grant, and the range ended early. The same tick opens the latch: a notice that arrives during a step's pause raises `TURN` above the step's entry stamp, which is the latch's only sign that a human answered, so a step could land with no one approving it. Once the card suggests a range at every boundary, long jobs inside ranges become the normal case.
  - *lean:* yes, as the build's first two steps: a spike that captures what the hook receives for a notice (step 1), then a fix that leaves `TURN` and `GRANT` alone for one (step 2), recorded as a plumbbob decision amending plumbbob [D64 (approval-latch)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d64) and plumbbob [D65 (human-typed-grants)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d65). If the spike finds nothing that tells a notice from a prompt, step 2 shrinks to the build skill telling the model to run long jobs in the foreground during a range, and the latch's exposure goes on record as an open risk.
- <a id="q2"></a>**Q2 (step-list-grants)**: *resolved:* 2026-10-17, the lean, recorded as [D12 (step-list-grants)](#d12); the question was whether a range can name a re-sequenced stretch, such as `20,5-9`
  - *plain:* A range names plan numbers and lands every step up to its top, so once the build order puts an appended step inside a stretch, no `N-M` describes it: checkride's 48-57 reached only 51 because step 76 was next, and fascicle's run of "steps 20 and 5 to 9" had to be typed as two. Without a fix, the card can suggest only ranges whose steps run in both numbering and build order, and has to split the rest, adding stops nobody asked for. Long builds, the ones ranges help most, are the ones refine and harvest re-sequence. The other fix, reading `N-M` as "through M in build order", is shorter to type, but it lets the model widen a grant by reordering the plan.
  - *lean:* let a range list its steps, numbers and spans joined by commas (`20,5-9`, `48-57,76`); the turn hook mints exactly that set and the latch lands only its members, *because* the set is fixed by what the human typed, so no later edit to the build order can widen it (plumbbob [D67 (auto-not-a-grant)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d67)). A plain `N-M` then grants exactly N through M, which is what plumbbob [D85 (range-top-lands)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d85) already says it does (the latch today allows any step under M). Steps 3 and 4 carry it; without them, step 6 reads `N` and `N-M` only, and [D10 (drift-reported)](#d10) covers a re-sequenced range.
- <a id="q3"></a>**Q3 (card-shape)**: *resolved:* 2026-10-17, the lean with [Q7 (range-model)](#q7)'s change to the model, recorded as [D13 (card-shape)](#d13), the exact strings left to step 5; the question was what the new lines say, and where they sit
  - *plain:* The card is the one surface the human reads every turn, and plumbbob [D80 (cli-renders-model-relays)](https://github.com/robmclarty/plumbbob/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d80) makes every line on it the CLI's, so each new line is a commitment: the relay check holds it, and the eval's anatomy reader reads it. The lines have to answer two questions without growing the card for a plan that has no ranges: what to type next, and, when a range has just ended, why the human is stopped here.
  - *lean:* three pieces. (1) The note beneath Next Up opens with the range holding the step Next Up names and the command that runs what is left of it, `· range 3 of 5: /plumbbob:build 6-9`, adding the range's model as `on Opus` when it differs from that step's, and reading `· range 3 of 5 needs a re-cut (step 76 sits inside it)` when [D10 (drift-reported)](#d10) flags it. - <a id="q4"></a>**Q4 (auto-and-stops)**: *resolved:* 2026-10-17, the lean, recorded as [D14 (auto-and-stops)](#d14); the question was whether `--auto` stops at a call the plan's ranges reserve for the human
  - *plain:* `--auto` is the human choosing to be away: it self-approves and chains to the end of the plan, halting only on trouble or a decision the plan never made. A bare range exists to put a call in front of the human before its step lands (said-once's step 5 settled the card's new label at its pause), and today an `--auto` run would make that call itself and keep going, because nothing tells it the plan reserved the call. A typed range is different: the human named the steps, so the steps are approved.
  - *lean:* yes, for a bare range only: under `--auto`, a step the ranges name as a bare range is built to its pause and halts there, at the decision tier, and `plumbbob build` says so as it enters the step (step 10). - <a id="q5"></a>**Q5 (notice-and-relay)**: *resolved:* 2026-10-17, the lean, recorded as [D15 (notice-clears-relay)](#d15); the question was how a notice's turn gets past the relay check once step 2 stops the notice ticking `TURN`
  - *plain:* `plumbbob relay` judges a turn only while the ending in `RELAY` was printed in the current `TURN` (`src/verbs/relay.ts:71`), and today the notice's own tick is what makes the last pause's ending stale on the notice's turn. - <a id="q6"></a>**Q6 (list-reading)**: *resolved:* 2026-10-17, the lean, recorded as [D16 (list-reading)](#d16); the question was how a list range reads when it is typed with a space after a comma, or written out of build order

## Prior art

The three range sections written on 2026-10-16, each by a model at Rob's request, partway through a build:

| Build | Section | Steps | Its shape | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| checkride, polyglot targets | `## Ranges` | 78 | a table: the command, its length, where it stops, and why | what each range should watch for; a lean written ahead for each call a range would otherwise stop to ask; what to do between ranges and after an early stop |
| fascicle, OpenRouter reasoning | `## Build runs` | 20 | a bullet per run: its steps and model, then a **Stop:** sentence | a run's model is the one its hardest step needs |
| plumbbob, said once, said whole | `## Build runs` | 17 | a table: the command, its model, where it stops, and why | optional splits that trade one more stop for a cheaper model |

All three say four things about a range: the command the human types, the model it needs, where it stops, and why, which is always a decision the plan left open or a review before later steps build on what landed. All three mark a decision with a bare single-step build, so the call is made at that step's own pause. Two of the three builds also show where ranges break today: a background job's notice cleared the grant partway (checkride at step 49, fascicle at step 11), and an appended step the build order placed inside a range cut it short or split it (checkride's 48-57, fascicle's "steps 20 and 5 to 9").

## Noticed, not in this build

- The plan pause's `looks good` could start range 1 when the plan declares one; today the human types it after the plan commit.
- `finish`'s report could tabulate the ranges: how long each ran, and where one stopped early.
- Checkride's section wrote a lean ahead for each call a range would otherwise stop to ask, and harvest folded each in once its range landed; a harvest that knew the ranges could propose those leans itself.
- An eval contract could hold `/plumbbob:plan` to a section that parses and names every step once.
